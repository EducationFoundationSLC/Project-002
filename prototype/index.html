Calculated line for line like an official TRIM notice.

Property Appraiser CSV exports include values and exemptions, but not tax rates. Project-002 combines CAMA records with county millage tables across three distinct taxable bases.

County Levies

County Taxable Value

Uses the roll's taxable value after all applicable exemptions—including Homestead and the county Senior (65+) exemption.

Cities & Independent Districts

Other Taxable Value

Adds back the county-only Senior exemption so municipal, fire, mosquito control, and water management levies match TRIM notices.

School Board

School Taxable Value

Applies only the first $25,000 Homestead exemption and excludes the 10% non-homestead assessment cap.

Verified Against 2026 TRIM

Rounded to the cent per levy.

Each taxing authority levy is rounded to the nearest cent before summing, then combined with non-ad valorem assessments. Note: City of Port St. Lucie solid waste fees (~$482) are not included in PASLC CSV exports and are noted separately.
