2026 Final Millage Rates

Calculated line for line from official county millage tables.

Live PASLC GIS records provide official appraised, assessed, exemption, and taxable values. Project-002 combines them with the 2026 Final Millage Rates across all 7 county tax districts.

County Levies

County Taxable Value

Applies General Fund, Fine & Forfeiture, Transit, Erosion, Mosquito Control, and MSTU levies to the parcel's taxable value after exemptions.

Cities & Independent Districts

Municipal & Fire Levies

Matches each parcel's tax district (0001, 0002, 0009, 0011, 0022, 0041, 0050) for Port St. Lucie, Fort Pierce, St. Lucie Village, Fire, SFWMD, and Children's Services.

School Board

School Millage (6.2450)

Combines School RLE (2.9970), Capital Outlay (1.5000), Discretionary (0.7480), and Voter Referendum (1.0000) levies.

Live Public GIS

Direct from map.paslc.gov

Queries Saint Lucie County's public ArcGIS parcel service directly—no local database build required to explore, map, or measure lots.

1. Parcel attributes and polygon boundaries are queried live from the Saint Lucie County Property Appraiser public GIS service (map.paslc.gov).
2. Ad valorem tax estimates use the 2026 Final Millage Rates from Project-002's tax_rates.csv. Official bills and non-ad valorem fees are published by the Property Appraiser and Tax Collector.
3. Lot drawings are measured from county GIS parcel boundaries and are for reference only, not a legal survey.
