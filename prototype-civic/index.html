2026 Final Millage Rates

Calculated line for line from official county millage tables.

Public PASLC records provide official appraised, assessed, exemption, and taxable values. Project-002 combines them with the 2026 Final Millage Rates across all 7 county tax districts.

County Levies

County Taxable Value

Applies General Fund, Fine & Forfeiture, Transit, Erosion, Mosquito Control, and MSTU levies to the parcel's taxable value after exemptions.

Cities & Independent Districts

Municipal & Fire Levies

Matches each parcel's tax district (0001, 0002, 0009, 0011, 0022, 0041, 0050) for Port St. Lucie, Fort Pierce, St. Lucie Village, Fire, SFWMD, and Children's Services.

School Board

School Millage (6.2450)

Combines School RLE (2.9970), Capital Outlay (1.5000), Discretionary (0.7480), and Voter Referendum (1.0000) levies.

Direct Official TRIM Access

Every parcel links to its official TRIM notice.

Click any property to open its detail sheet and view its official Saint Lucie County Property Appraiser TRIM PDF directly from paslc.gov. Note: Non-ad valorem assessments (such as PSL solid waste and stormwater fees) appear on the official TRIM notice and are included automatically when running with the local SQLite database.

Dual-Mode Architecture

Local SQLite or Live GIS

Automatically connects to your local Project-002 Python/SQLite backend when served via server.py, or queries map.paslc.gov live when opened standalone.

1. Parcel attributes and polygon boundaries are sourced from the Saint Lucie County Property Appraiser public GIS service (map.paslc.gov) or your local Project-002 SQLite database.

2. Ad valorem tax estimates use the 2026 Final Millage Rates from Project-002's tax_rates.csv. Official tax bills and non-ad valorem assessments are published by the Property Appraiser and Tax Collector.

3. Lot drawings are measured from county GIS parcel boundaries and are for reference only, not a legal survey.
